The Spiral Algorithm

Simulates the Caesar cipher + deck stacking mechanism found in NY voter rolls. CID numbers are divided into strips by digit length, each strip is cut and reordered (Caesar cipher), then all strips are interlaced using repunit spacing. Consecutive SBOEID numbers are assigned to the result.

Covert Tagging Mechanism

Fraudulent records are injected alongside legitimate ones. The fraud signal is not in the numbers themselves — it is in which SBOEID is paired with which CID. Records in the anchor strip (strip 0) are the tagged fraudulent records. Without the algorithm key, all records look identical.

Records — with vs. without the algorithm key

Tartan — Out of Range (OOR) Partition

The OOR partition uses a different algorithm nicknamed "Tartan". A scatterplot of SBOEID (Y) vs CID (X) reveals two formations: columns (active records) and slabs (purged at creation). The paper asks: how did the system know these records were ineligible the moment they were assigned numbers?
